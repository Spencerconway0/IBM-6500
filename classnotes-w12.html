Case 31 slide review

  • We spent a good part of class reviewing the Case 31 analysis slides and talking through why we use different tests for different research questions.
  • R3 (employee proficiency): Q3–Q6 were combined into one score after checking that the items measured the same thing and worked reliably together. The average was 4.21, which was significantly above 4 in the one-sample t-test.
  • R4 (near headquarters vs. elsewhere): We used an independent-samples t-test to compare employee proficiency scores across the two groups. For service awareness, we used cross-tabs and a chi-square test of independence.
  • R6 (operational effectiveness): The test depended on the type of response. Chi-square goodness-of-fit was used for counts, and a one-sample t-test was used for ratings.
  • H1 and H2: H1 used one-way ANOVA to compare how members rated the adequacy of financial services across awareness groups. H2 used a paired t-test because the same members answered both loan-rate questions.
