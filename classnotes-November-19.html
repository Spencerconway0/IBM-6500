SEM

  • SEM stands for structural equation modeling.
  • It looks at relationships among several variables in one model.
  • The measurement model looks at how survey questions measure concepts, such as satisfaction.
  • The structural model looks at the proposed relationships between those concepts.
  • This connects to CFA because we need to check our measures before interpreting the relationships.
